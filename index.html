03 / A useful process

Turn a list
into a shortlist.

A practical Kakobuy spreadsheet guide starts with a consistent method. Save enough information to understand what you are comparing and what still needs confirmation.

For Kakobuy clothing finds and sneaker finds alike, a descriptive note is more useful than an unsupported “best buy” label.

  1. Choose a category and save the original link.

    Browse the category that fits your needs. Record the seller’s original listing URL, product name and selected variant so you can find the same item again.

  2. Compare the specific listing.

    Check the size chart, material description, color selection and available photos. Similar-looking items can have different measurements and construction.

  3. Estimate the full landed cost.

    Add item cost, domestic delivery, service fees, international shipping and applicable taxes. Include the currency and the date of your estimate.

  4. Confirm before ordering.

    Recheck stock, return terms and service compatibility. Confirm product and destination restrictions with your selected service; this page does not verify purchasing eligibility.

05 / Straight answers

Before you
click through.

Quick answers for readers researching a Kakobuy spreadsheet, product links and shopping checks.

What is a Kakobuy spreadsheet?

A Kakobuy spreadsheet is a product research list used to organize shopping links, categories, sizes, prices and notes. A spreadsheet entry is a starting point for comparison, not a guarantee of stock, authenticity or service compatibility.

Is this an official Kakobuy website?

No. This is an independent browsing guide that links to CAFANS. It is not operated by Kakobuy or the fashion brands shown.

Can every CAFANS product link be used with Kakobuy?

Compatibility is not verified here. Check the original seller link and confirm that your selected shopping service currently supports the seller, product and destination before ordering.

How should I compare sizes?

Use the specific listing’s measurement chart and compare it with an item you already own. For clothing, compare chest, length, shoulder and waist measurements. For shoes, compare foot length and the model-specific size chart.

What does QC mean in a shopping spreadsheet?

QC means quality control. Inspection photos can help you check the selected item, visible condition, measurements and obvious differences from the listing. Photos alone do not authenticate a branded product.

Does the listed price include international shipping?

Do not assume that it does. Confirm item cost, domestic delivery, service fees, international shipping, currency conversion and applicable duties or taxes separately.
